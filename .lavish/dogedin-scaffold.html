1 · Architecture decision: who owns commerce

You chose Shopify owns commerce. Since products already live in Shopify, its Storefront API is the single source of truth for catalog, pricing, discounts, cart, and checkout. Supabase is wired up but reserved for what Shopify doesn't own.

🛍️ Shopify Storefront API

Source of truth for commerce.

  • Products, images, descriptions
  • Price + compare-at price (discounts)
  • Cart & hosted checkout (no payments to rebuild)

lib/shopify.ts · thin fetch, no SDK

🗄️ Supabase

Custom content Shopify can't hold.

  • Carousel CMS entries (later)
  • Newsletter / waitlist signups (later)
  • Idle today - no tables, no duplicate inventory

lib/supabase.ts · null until env set

Why not Supabase for commerce? It would mean two sources of truth to reconcile and re-implementing checkout/payments - heavier and more fragile for zero gain while Shopify already does it.
