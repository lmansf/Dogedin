# Dogedin Award/Recognition Opportunities — Research Findings (verified July 4, 2026)

## RECOMMENDATION: GO — Better Cities For Pets city certification, starting now

It is the only opportunity that is: open today, free, explicitly allows a community partner (not city government) to drive the application, near-certain to succeed given Dunedin's credentials, and structurally perfect for Dogedin's brand ("the site named Dogedin got Dogedin certified"). Tampa and St. Pete are certified while famously dog-obsessed Dunedin is not. Winning it also unlocks the annual $20K BCFP grant track, and the mayor's-office relationship it requires is reusable for the PetSafe park grants (June 2027) and a HABRI nomination (May 2027).

Sequenced plan: BCFP now → DWAA entries Sept–Oct 2026 → HABRI + Best of the Bay/Best of the Best + PetSafe in spring 2027.

## VERIFIED FINDINGS

### 1. Better Cities For Pets™ City Certification (Mars Petcare) — THE opportunity
- **Who runs it:** Mars Petcare (bettercitiesforpets.com)
- **Dunedin's status:** NOT certified. St. Petersburg (2019) and Tampa (2025) are certified; Dunedin is absent. 100+ cities certified across 32 states.
- **Eligibility (key finding):** The assessment "should be completed by someone within city government **or a designated partner, such as a local nonprofit or animal welfare organization**" — submitter must affirm the Mayor is aware and supportive. Dogedin can drive this; it needs a nod from City Hall, not a council vote.
- **Deadline/cadence:** Rolling, no deadline. Free. ~1 hour for the first assessment pass, review in 1–2 weeks, certification lasts 5 years.
- **Effort:** Low-moderate — gather city pet data, get mayor's office buy-in, submit online.
- **Odds:** High. Cities need only be "taking action on at least one pet-related priority." Dunedin (Honeymoon Island dog beach, dog-friendly Main Street, Dogtoberfest, Highlander dog park) clears the bar easily.
- **Bonus:** Certification (or even completing the assessment) unlocks eligibility for annual BCFP grants — five $20,000 grants (annual cycle; 2024 apps closed Aug 16).
- **Marketing value:** Massive either way — "Dogedin got Dunedin certified" is a press release, a city-commission photo op, and permanent site content.
- Sources: https://www.bettercitiesforpets.com/resource/how-to-apply-for-better-cities-for-pets-city-certification/ , https://www.bettercitiesforpets.com/certified-cities-landing/ , https://www.bettercitiesforpets.com/grants/

### 2. HABRI Human Animal Bond Innovation Awards — best national fit for Dogedin itself
- Human Animal Bond Research Institute; free public online nominations.
- Categories include "Pet-Inclusive Culture", "Public Service", "Technology Innovation" — a community platform fits.
- 2026 nominations closed May 22, 2026. **Next cycle: spring 2027.** Effort: low. Odds: moderate.
- Source: https://habri.org/hab-innovation-awards

### 3. PetSafe "Bark for Your Park" Grants (+ Unleashed contest) — organize-the-town play
- 25 community awards/yr: five $25K (new parks), ten $10K (enhance), ten $5K (maintain). Applicants must be nonprofits or local governments — Dogedin can't apply alone but can organize Dunedin's entry with Parks & Rec. **2026 deadline passed June 30; annual — target 2027.** The companion "Unleashed" contest ($50K park, national daily public voting) is tailor-made for a community platform to run the vote drive.
- Sources: https://www.petsafe.com/blog/more-dog-parks-more-fun-with-new-bark-for-your-park-grants/

### 4. DWAA Maxwell Medallion (Dog Writers Association of America) — nearest actionable deadline
- Annual national writing competition, open to non-members; categories include blog postings/online articles. Entries typically **Sept 1–Oct 31** for work published Sep–Aug; $7,000+ in cash/special awards. Dogedin's best content could be entered **this September**. Modest per-entry fee (confirm at dogwriters.org/writing-competition). Odds: moderate; a nomination = "award-winning" badge.

### 5. Creative Loafing "Best of the Bay" (Tampa Bay) — free local ballot
- 500+ reader-ballot categories incl. pet categories (Woof Gang Bakery's Dunedin location won "Best Bakery for Your Pet" 2025). Free nominations at vote.cltampa.com. 2026 noms closed June 14; **next nominations ~May 2027.** Odds depend on vote mobilization — which a community platform is built for.

### 6. Tampa Bay Times "Best of the Best" — same idea, bigger paper
- Community-nominated, vote-based; covers businesses, groups, locations, services. **Next cycle: April 2027.** Free.
- Source: https://tampabaybestofthebest.com/

### 7. Purina Pet Care Innovation Prize — only if Dogedin frames itself as a startup
- $25K non-dilutive + accelerator, 5 winners/yr from ~200 applicants; 2027 applications open at SuperZoo, Aug 2026. Weak fit unless there's a revenue/product story.

### 8. Smaller/lower-priority verified options
- **Nextdoor Neighborhood Faves** — free, annual, needs a claimed Nextdoor Business Page. Passive lottery ticket.
- **Dunedin Chamber of Commerce annual awards** — categories not published online; membership likely required — worth one phone call to 301 Main St.
- **Pet Innovation Awards (petinnovationawards.com)** — pay-to-enter marketing-badge program. Cheap credibility if the fee is small; low prestige.

## DISCARDED (investigated, not viable)

- Awesome Foundation Tampa Bay $1K micro-grants — chapter inactive.
- USA Today 10Best "dog-friendly" awards — editorially nominated, no application path.
- City of Dunedin grants — CRA incentives target physical/downtown businesses.
- "Dog Town USA" contest — no currently operating contest verified.
